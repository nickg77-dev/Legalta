# Contacts and compliance

## Who to find (2 to 3 people per company)
| Segment | Decision-maker | Records holder | Influencer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner-run private fleet (10-50 trucks) | Owner or managing director | Finance or office manager | Transport manager |
| Mid-size fleet (50-200 trucks) | Finance director or CFO | Fleet or transport manager | Managing director |
| Public body | Finance or commercial director (never the mayor) | Fleet or depot manager | Legal or procurement lead |
| Intermediary (association, dealer) | Chief executive or director | Membership or events lead | Board member |

## Free sources, in this order (no paid contact databases)
1. Registers that publish names: GB operator licence file (directors, transport manager), company registers (officers).
2. Company website: team page, imprint or legal notice, contact page (generic email, switchboard). Required for every In batch row: one Phone value (or "none found") and both Contact email cells (a found address, or the "@domain" prefill). Rules in SKILL.md 7a.
3. LinkedIn profile URLs via Linked Helper exports (a human runs Linked Helper; Claude reads the export).
4. Switchboard numbers via Google Places (places_search).
5. Email pattern: infer from a published named email at the same domain; write "pattern" in Research notes until checked. With no confirmed pattern, prefill "@domain" instead of guessing.

## Compliance checks before any contact is written to the CRM
- Record source and date for every detail, plus a lawful basis note (legitimate interest, B2B).
- UK PECR: unsolicited marketing email only to corporate subscribers. Sole traders and partnerships count as individuals: postal letter or phone instead, unless consent exists.
- UK phone: screen against TPS and CTPS before calling.
- EU: legitimate-interest assessment; honour objections immediately.
- Opt-out on file: never contact again. Hard stop.
- Mobile numbers only from a lawful published source.
