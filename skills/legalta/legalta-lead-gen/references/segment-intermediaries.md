# Segment playbook - intermediaries (associations, dealers, fleet maintenance, accountants)

Why first: one intermediary reaches tens or hundreds of fleets. On the Compensation Stage Table, warm leads earn about EUR 40 per hour against about EUR 10 for cold leads (Nick's estimate).

## Types and what to ask for
| Type | Examples | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Own-account sector associations | UKIFDA, SIBA, Cold Chain Federation, Craft Bakers | Member briefing, newsletter item, webinar, member-only page |
| Haulage associations | Logistics UK, ANITA, CETM, TLN, UND | Same; check first whether they already run a claim (COMPETITOR flag) |
| Truck dealers and fleet maintenance chains | Exhibitors at CV Show, Transpotec, Solutrans | Referral of 1998-2012 buyers; they hold sales records |
| Fleet accountants and consultants | Firms serving hauliers | Client introductions |

## Rules
- Check the Saturation register: an association running its own claim is a competitor, not a channel.
- Never imply endorsement by an association that has not agreed to it.
- Log every intermediary as its own Lead row (Lead Type: Intermediary), with members reached as an estimate.
