## How Authority is scored

Authority (1 to 5) = average of Reach and Signal, to the nearest 0.5.

- **Reach** = largest single channel (newsletter or social), conservative figure. 1: under 10K. 2: 10K to 50K. 3: 50K to 200K. 4: 200K to 1M. 5: 1M or more.
- **Signal** = how much their Claude advice is worth per word. 5: insider or published research. 4: publishes tested results or real costs. 3: experienced practitioner or educator. 2: general adoption advice. 1: news, hype or course marketing.
- **Heavyweight** = Nick's call, shown separately from the formula: Boris Cherny, Ethan Mollick, Ruben Hassid.
- Authority orders the consensus table and breaks ties. It never overrules evidence type (SKILL.md weighting rules 1 to 3).

Link status: VERIFIED (resolved 14 Oct 2026), INFERRED (from Gmail sender or a standard URL pattern).
